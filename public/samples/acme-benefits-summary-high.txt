Lincoln DentalConnect High — Benefits Summary
Acme Corp · Group 00412345 · Plan year: calendar year (January 1 – December 31)
For eligible employees and enrolled dependents. This summary describes your dental benefits.

Enrollment Summary
Your monthly premium: $48.00, deducted pre-tax from your paycheck.
Network: Lincoln DentalConnect PPO. You may also see dentists outside the network (see §3).

Schedule of Benefits, §1  Annual Deductible
Annual deductible: $50 per person per calendar year. Applies to Basic and Major services.
Preventive services are not subject to the deductible.

Schedule of Benefits, §1.4  Q4 Deductible Carryover
Q4 carryover: any deductible met in October, November or December also counts toward next year's deductible.

Schedule of Benefits, §2  Coinsurance (what the plan pays after the deductible)
Service class             In-network      Out-of-network
Preventive & Diagnostic   100%            100%
Basic                     90%             80%
Major                     60%             50%
Orthodontia               50%             50%

Out-of-Network Benefits, §3  Dentists Outside the Network
Out-of-network claims are reimbursed at the 90th percentile of usual and customary (U&C) charges.
You pay the difference between the dentist's bill and the plan's allowance.

Plan Maximums, §4  Annual and Lifetime Maximums
Annual maximum benefit: $2,000 per person per calendar year, in and out of network combined.
Orthodontic lifetime maximum: $1,500 per person.

Plan Maximums, §4.2  Preventive Care
Preventive care does not count toward the annual maximum (SmileRewards).

Lincoln MaxRewards, §5  Rollover for Low-Use Years
If your plan-paid claims for a calendar year total $1,000 or less, $500 rolls over to next year's maximum.
If every claim that year was in network, an extra $150 is added to the rollover.
Your MaxRewards rollover account can hold up to $2,000.
Rollover amounts are deposited on day 65 of the following plan year.

Eligibility, §6  Waiting Periods
Waiting periods: None. Coverage for every service class begins on your effective date.

Limitations, §8  Frequency Limits
Exams: 2 per calendar year
Cleanings: 2 per calendar year
Bitewing X-rays: 1 per calendar year
Full-mouth or panoramic X-rays: 1 per 60 months
Crowns and bridges: 1 per tooth per 60 months
Buildups and posts: 1 per tooth per 60 months
Scaling and root planing: 1 per quadrant per 24 months
Night guards: 1 per 36 months

Limitations, §8.3  Alternate Benefit
No alternate benefit: tooth-colored fillings on back teeth are paid at the composite rate.

Work in Progress, §9  Crowns and Bridges
A crown or bridge is payable on the date of preparation, not the date it is seated.
