## Highest-value improvements

1. **Redact production logs.** Remove member/claim/connection identifiers, filenames and raw provider exceptions from log payloads; log bounded event codes and failure classes. Check logging configuration and capture tests, and make a clear retention note. Raising Principles from 2 to 4 would add **10 points**, subject to a fresh audit.
2. **Obtain a usable approved fee dataset.** Preserve code/variant, region, date and billed-vs-allowed distinction, with confirmed plan/network-specific contracted fees and exact UCR/MAC amounts. Replace synthetic defaults deliberately; imported tables must not be shadowed by existing procedure overrides. Published clinic charges cannot establish insurer reimbursement. Raising cost grounding and network comparison from 2 to 4 would add **3.15 points**.
3. **Explain every recommended procedure date.** Display the binding frequency/eligibility constraint, annual-max balance before/after, deductible reset/carryover effect and current-versus-proposed member cost, preserving clinical deadlines. Raising sequencing from 2 to 4 would add **3 points**.
4. **Make output limits explicit and contextual.** State “This estimate does not replace your plan document or your dentist's treatment plan,” and distinguish confirmed plan terms, quotes, reference estimates and provisional full-fee budgets beside amounts, not solely in a footer. Raising this criterion from 2 to 4 would add **1.8 points**.
5. **Strengthen explanation/retry details.** Derive deductible wording from actual applicable classes, require accurate per-step amount roles/percentages and citations, add bounded OCR deadlines and retry-with-saved-file behavior, and show the running maximum after each visit. The corresponding remaining score headroom is **3.825 points**.

These are proposed improvements from the assessment, not changes claimed to have been implemented in this Docker task. Their point estimates assume judges agree with both the current and improved anchors.
